# Consumer process checklist

A plain-language checklist of the SCVRD vocational rehabilitation process,
grounded in the Consumer Services Policies. Give this to someone starting
(or in the middle of) services.

## Before you start

- [ ] **Apply.** Contact SCVRD and submit an application. A counselor will
  be assigned to you.
- [ ] **Eligibility.** The counselor decides if you are eligible for
  services and sets your priority (CSP 2.20). They may ask for medical or
  school records — only what is needed to make that decision (CSP 4.40).

## Your plan

- [ ] **Build the IPE together.** You and the counselor write your
  Individualized Plan for Employment: your job goal, the services you need,
  start dates, and who provides each one (CSP 2.40).
- [ ] **Use your informed choice.** Ask questions about every option. The
  counselor must give you counseling and guidance so you can decide —
  nobody decides for you (CSP 1.10, CSP 4.20).
- [ ] **Know your money rules.** Ask whether each service needs a financial
  need test (form SCVR  4b). Counseling, assessments, job placement help,
  and several other services are always free of the test (CSP 3.10). If you
  get SSI, SSDI, SNAP, or TANF — or your household income is at or below
  300% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines — you are exempt from paying.
- [ ] **Check other programs first.** Ask what comparable services you
  should apply for (for example, Medicaid or a school program) before SCVRD
  pays (CSP 3.20).

## While you get services

- [ ] **Update the IPE when plans change.** New service, new goal, or help
  after you start a job — it all goes through an IPE amendment (CSP 2.40,
  CSP 2.50).
- [ ] **Keep your records.** Keep copies of your IPE, the financial need
  form (SCVR 4b), receipts, and letters. SCVRD keeps a case file on you too
  (CSP 2.01) — you can ask what's in it.

## If you disagree with a decision

- [ ] **Speak up early.** Tell your counselor what you disagree with and
  ask for the reason in writing.
- [ ] **Ask for an Area Supervisor review.**
- [ ] **Contact the Client Assistance Program (CAP)** — free, independent
  help for VR applicants and consumers.
- [ ] **Ask about mediation or an impartial hearing.**
- These steps come from the rights policy (CSP 1.20), and the funding
  disputes section of CSP 3.10 points to the same chain.
