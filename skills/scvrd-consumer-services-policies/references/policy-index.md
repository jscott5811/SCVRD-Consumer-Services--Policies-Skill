# Policy index

The Consumer Services Policies manual is organized in four sections.
Descriptions below are one-line paraphrases; read the linked policy for the
full text. Manual page (rev. 07-2025):
https://www.scvrd.net/238/Consumer-Services-Policies

## Section 1 — How the manual works / rights

| ID | Title | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| CSP 1.10 | VR Policy Formulation and Implementation | The manual itself: SCVRD writes policies for every VR service; services are based on the consumer's IPE and informed choice. [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/419/CSP-110-VR-Policy-Formulation-and-Implementation) |
| CSP 1.20 | Applicant and Consumer Rights | Rights of applicants and consumers, including the grievance/appeal chain. Cited by CSP 3.10 §3.6.C. (Find on the manual page.) |

## Section 2 — The case process

| ID | Title | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| CSP 2.01 | Case Files | What SCVRD must keep in each person's case file, including eligibility and closure records. [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/452/CSP-201-Case-Files) |
| CSP 2.20 | Eligibility Determination | How the counselor decides eligibility and priority for services. Cited by CSP 4.40 §3.1. (Find on the manual page.) |
| CSP 2.40 | Individualized Plan for Employment | How the IPE is built, what it must contain, and what goals are not allowed (e.g., unpaid homemaker outcomes). [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/410/CSP-240-Individualized-Plan-for-Employment) |
| CSP 2.50 | Post-Employment Services | Help keeping or advancing in a job after employment starts but before closure; limited scope and duration, needs an IPE amendment; after exit, the person must reapply. [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/411/CSP-250-Post-Employment-Services) |

## Section 3 — Money

| ID | Title | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| CSP 3.10 | Analysis of Financial Need | The financial test (form SCVR 4b): which services are exempt, who is exempt (SSI/SSDI/SNAP/TANF or ≤300% of Federal Poverty Guidelines), how the consumer's share is calculated, and yearly reviews. [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/459/CSP-310-Analysis-of-Financial-Need) |
| CSP 3.20 | Comparable Services and Benefits | Using other programs first before SCVRD pays; the services exempt from this rule (assessments, counseling and guidance, job-related services, rehab technology, post-employment). [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/439/CSP-320-Comparable-Services-and-Benefits) |

## Section 4 — Services

| ID | Title | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| CSP 4.20 | Vocational Rehabilitation Counseling and Guidance | Career counseling throughout the whole case; provided by SCVRD, not purchased outside. [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/383/CSP-420-Vocational-Rehabilitation-Counseling-and-Guidance) |
| CSP 4.22 | Information and Referral Services | Help connecting to other agencies and the workforce system when SCVRD can't provide a needed service. [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/385/CSP-422-Information-and-Referral-Services) |
| CSP 4.31 | Supported Employment | Long-term, on-the-job support for people with the most significant disabilities (serious limits in 3+ daily-living areas, multiple services over 12+ months). [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/152/Supported-Employment-Services-PDF?bidId=) |
| CSP 4.40 | Diagnosis and Treatment of Impairments | Medical evaluations, therapies, and related treatment tied to the employment goal; evaluations for applicants only as needed for eligibility/priority. [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/433/CSP-440-Diagnosis-and-Treatment-of-Impairments) |
| CSP 4.51 | Job Placement Assistance | Help getting into competitive integrated employment; placement activities start only after the service is on the IPE. [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/431/CSP-451-Job-Placement-Assistance) |
| CSP 4.52 | Self-Employment Services | Three tiers: freelance/gig work, sole proprietor, and business ownership; must fit the consumer's informed choice and strengths. [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/150/Self-Employment-Services-PDF) |
| CSP 4.82 | Transportation Assistance | Rides, gas, and travel support for assessments and IPE services; vehicle modification is rehab technology, not transportation. [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/422/CSP-482-Transportation-Assistance) |
| CSP 4.83 | Services to Consumer's Family Members | Services for family members when needed for the consumer's job outcome; must be on the IPE. [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/421/CSP-483-Services-to-Consumers-Family-Members) |
| CSP 4.84 | Other Services | Goods and services not covered elsewhere that help the job goal (e.g., occupational licenses, tools). [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/399/CSP-484-Other-Services) |
| CSP 4.91 | Credential Attainment | Which earned credentials count (degrees, licenses) and which don't (special-education certificates, first aid/CPR, keyboarding, etc.). [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/450/CSP-491-Credential-Attainment) |
| CSP 4.93 | Disability Related Skills Training | Training in disability-management skills (orientation and mobility, rehab teaching, sign language, cognitive training, etc.) tied to the job goal. [Link](https://www.scvrd.net/DocumentCenter/View/371/CSP-493-Disability-Related-Skills-Training) |
| CSP 4.99 | Postsecondary Training for Degrees | How SCVRD pays for degree programs at colleges and universities. Cited by CSP 3.10 §3.5. (Find on the manual page.) |
| CSP 4.991 | Training for Non-Degree Credentials | How SCVRD pays for non-degree job training. Cited by CSP 3.10 §3.5. (Find on the manual page.) |

> Note: the index covers the policies verified during the skill build. The
